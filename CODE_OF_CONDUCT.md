# Code of conduct

Kakangkung is made by people from many places and backgrounds. We want everyone to feel welcome
to use it, ask questions and contribute.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent; ask before you assume.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Credit others for their ideas and work.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour of any kind.
- Sharing other people's private information.
- Spam, trolling, or derailing discussions on purpose.

## Where it applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere you represent the
project.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/revanapriyandi) or the contact in the maintainer's profile. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and block people who do not follow this
code.
